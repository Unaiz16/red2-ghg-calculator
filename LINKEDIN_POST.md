Why can a fuel be 99% bio and still save 'only' around 80% of greenhouse gas emissions?

Because the two numbers answer different questions. Bio content says where the carbon comes from. GHG savings compare the emissions of the whole supply chain with a fossil reference of 94 gCO2eq/MJ. Cultivation, processing and transport all count.

I have years of experience leading production and procurement at a renewable fuels producer, where I raised the GHG savings of the company's fuels to 78% (RED II methodology) by sourcing bio-based feedstocks. So I built a free tool that shows the calculation.

It covers biofuels, SAF and e-fuels under the EU RED. Enter your values or pick a default pathway, and it checks the result against the required minimum. You can compare supplier offers, every field has a short help text, and it works in English and German.

It is an educational tool, not for certification.

Try it here: https://red2-ghg-calculator.pages.dev

I am a chemical engineer in Berlin, Environmental Protection Officer (TÜV Rheinland) and IPMA Level D certified project manager.
