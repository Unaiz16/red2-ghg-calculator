Why can a fuel be 99% bio and still save 'only' around 80% of greenhouse gas emissions?

Because the two numbers answer different questions. Bio content says where the carbon comes from. GHG savings compare the emissions of the whole supply chain with a fossil reference of 94 gCO2eq/MJ, and cultivation, processing and transport all count.

I spent four years leading production and procurement at a renewable fuels producer. In that time I raised the GHG savings of the company's fuels to 78% (RED II methodology) by sourcing bio-based feedstocks. So I built a small free tool that shows the calculation.

You enter the emission terms or pick a default pathway. It gives you the savings, checks them against the threshold for the plant's start date, and explains each term in plain language.

It is an educational tool, not for certification or audits.

Try it here: https://red2-ghg-calculator.pages.dev

I am a chemical engineer in Berlin, Environmental Protection Officer (TÜV Rheinland) and IPMA Level D certified project manager.
